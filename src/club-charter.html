Our Book Club Charter

Short enough to fit on one page, long enough to cover the fights we intend to never have.

Club name
Founded in / around

Our purpose: We gather to read one book at a time in good company — to be curious together, to disagree like grown-ups, and to never leave anyone’s snack behind.

The deal, in seven lines

  • What we read: anything a member picks — fiction, nonfiction, cookbook, spacecraft manual. Quality varies. Love endures.
  • When we meet: monthly-ish, and we skip politely when life happens.
  • Host rotates: the picker hosts (or passes); hosting means a room, some snacks, and zero obligation to a three course meal.
  • Pick rotates: no one picks twice in a row; the tracker sheet keeps us honest.
  • Spoiler policy: past the stop-at page we mark no spoilers. If in doubt, wave it off and wait.
  • Disagreement is welcome: argue with the book, not the people. Everyone’s hot take is trying its best.
  • Attendance is not homework: you’re welcome if you skimmed, stalled, or came hungry. No tests are given.

We also honor

  • Check-in and check-out sentences for everyone
  • Remote members get full voice, not guest status
  • The cheddar clause (see snack sheet)

Amendments

Any member may propose a change; the club votes; snacks are consumed while we wait.

Signed by the founding members
