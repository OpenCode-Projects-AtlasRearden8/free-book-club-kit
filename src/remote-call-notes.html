Host etiquette for the call

  • Camera sits where the whole table can be seen
  • Announce names before people talk
  • One mic rule at the table
  • Quiet catch-up aside: stay on-topic by voice

Five minutes before

  • Dial in early — test the sound
  • Laptop charged + plugged in
  • If it fails: call by phone, book be darned
  • “We missed you” saved for the goodnight
