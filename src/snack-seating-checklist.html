Free Book Club Kit

Snack & Seating Checklist

People can push through a slow chapter; nobody pushes through a rock-hard chair. This page looks after both.

Snacks

  • Crunchy thing (pretzels, chips, carrots)
  • Soft-sweet thing (cookies, clementines)
  • One “theme” snack tied to the book, if fun
  • Napkins — talk-and-eat insurance
  • Water for everyone + a refill pitcher
  • Tea or coffee for the tea-and-coffee people
  • Checked member-card allergy notes
  • Vegetarian / gluten-free option ready
  • Nothing garlicky enough to liven up the debate too much

Seating & room

  • Seat for every confirmed member
  • One cushiony bonus seat for a plus-one
  • Floor spot with a pillow for overflow seating
  • Lamps on, overhead light off (or down)
  • Table flat enough for books & mugs
  • Music off, notifications on silent
  • Book piles shuffled so no one squints
  • Wi-Fi password written where guests sit (remote-friendly)
  • Remote member’s view: light on face, sound on
  • Tissues out (a good book can do that)

The night-of sweep

  • Glasses, plates, popcorn bits corralled
  • Timer or candle moment — set the tone
Snack budget or costs to split

Zero-effort fallbacks

No time to shop? Crackers + a sharp cheddar is a perfectly good club snack. Team has never once complained. The book is the point; the cheddar is the garnish.
